# Playthrough

Playthrough is a music tracking app built around albums, not just songs. It connects to Spotify and Last.fm, tracks what users are listening to, and turns that listening history into album completion progress.

The goal is simple: help people see which albums they have fully played through, which albums they are close to finishing, and which completed albums they want to show off on a personal wall.

## What It Does

Playthrough watches a user's recent listening activity and organizes it by album. Instead of only showing a stream of tracks, it answers questions like:

- Which albums have I completed?
- Which albums am I halfway through?
- What tracks are left on this album?
- What albums do I want to save to my wall?
- What are my friends putting on their walls?

## Key Features

### Album Completion Tracking

Playthrough calculates album progress based on listened tracks. Users can browse albums with completion percentages, track counts, and cover art.

### Unfinished Albums

Users can filter for albums they have started but not finished. This makes it easy to pick up where they left off and finish albums that are already in progress.

### Track-Level Progress

Each album can be opened to see its track list and which tracks have already been heard. This makes completion feel concrete instead of just being a percentage.

### Completed Album Archive

When a Last.fm album reaches 100%, Playthrough keeps that completion saved for the user. Monthly listening data can reset, but completed albums remain available as accomplishments and wall candidates.

### Personal Album Wall

Users can add completed albums to a personal wall. The wall acts like a curated shelf of albums they have finished and want to display.

### Shared Walls

Playthrough includes shared wall views so users can browse other people's album walls and see what they have completed or chosen to feature.

### Recent Listening

Users can view recent listening activity and currently playing music, giving the app a live connection to their listening habits.

### Spotify and Last.fm Support

Playthrough supports both Spotify and Last.fm flows, making it useful for people who track music through either service.

## Why It Exists

Most music apps are optimized around songs, playlists, and short-term listening history. Playthrough is designed for album listeners: people who care about finishing records, comparing progress, and building a collection of completed albums.

It turns passive listening history into a sense of progress, completion, and personal curation.
